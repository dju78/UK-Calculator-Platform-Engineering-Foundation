# Commercial & Monetisation Policy

This policy governs all existing, planned, and future commercial activities on the UK Calculator Platform. It guarantees that commercialisation will never compromise mathematical correctness, editorial independence, user privacy, or public trust.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **User Usefulness & Accuracy First:** Our primary mission is providing dependable, accurate, and accessible calculation tools for the UK public.
2. **Mathematical & Editorial Independence:** Calculation formulas, mathematical algorithms, tax rules, and statutory assumptions are independent and can **never** be altered, weighted, or influenced by sponsors, advertisers, or commercial partners.
3. **Transparent & Unambiguous Labelling:** Any future paid placement, sponsored tool, or commercial affiliate link must be explicitly marked (e.g. &ldquo;Advertisement&rdquo; or &ldquo;Sponsored&rdquo;).
4. **Rel Sponsored & Nofollow:** All commercial and affiliate outbound links must carry `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`.
5. **No Regulated Financial Advice:** The platform provides general illustrative tools and does not offer regulated financial, investment, pension, or mortgage advice under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
6. **Clinical & Health Protections:** Health, reproductive, pregnancy, and debt insolvency tools are protected from intrusive or predatory commercial monetization.
7. **No Deceptive UX or Dark Patterns:**
   - Commercial elements must never obscure or cover calculation results.
   - Commercial elements must never block the &ldquo;Calculate&rdquo; or reset buttons.
   - Ads must never masquerade as calculation recommendations or statutory output notes.
   - No misleading urgency, fake countdown timers, or artificial scarcity.
   - No forced email collection or paywalls before accessing basic calculations.
8. **Privacy & Data Isolation:** User inputs, financial sums, salaries, and calculation results are **never** shared with ad networks, data brokers, or commercial affiliates.
9. **Global Kill Switch:** The platform engineering foundation maintains environment-level kill switches (`NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_ADS`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_AFFILIATES`) capable of disabling all commercial slots instantly.

## 2. Category-Specific Monetisation Boundaries

| Category Family | Advertising Permitted? | Affiliate Permitted? | Governance Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Health & Fitness** | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | Clinical and body-composition tools must remain completely non-commercial. |
| **UK Tax & Salary** | Permitted (Generic display only) | Prohibited | Tax estimates must remain impartial; no pay-to-win accounting referrals. |
| **Pensions & Retirement** | Permitted (Generic display only) | Disclosed partnerships only | Must carry clear non-advice notices; no guaranteed annuity promotions. |
| **Mortgages & Property** | Permitted (Display) | Disclosed broker comparison | No fabricated APRs, rates, or lending criteria. |
| **Business & Commercial** | Permitted | Disclosed B2B software | Relevant tooling (e.g. accounting software, payroll integration). |
| **Everyday & Utilities** | Permitted | Permitted | Standard non-intrusive display. |

---

## 3. Governance & Review Process

Any prospective commercial arrangement or advertising network integration must undergo formal review against this policy before activation. If a commercial agreement creates a conflict of interest with calculation integrity, the agreement will be rejected or terminated.
