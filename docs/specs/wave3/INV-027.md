## Purpose

Calculate asset buy and sell orders to realign an investment portfolio to target allocation percentages.

## Scope

Multi-asset portfolio management supporting cash flow injections, withdrawals, and full rebalancing.

## Assumptions

- Target allocations sum to 100%.
- New cash deposits can be directed to underweight assets.
- Withdrawals funded from overweight assets.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Target percentages must sum to 100% (+/- 0.5%).
- Asset values must be >= 0.
- Total portfolio value + cash flow > 0.

## Formula and logic

V_new = sum(Current_i) + CashFlow. Target_i = V_new * TargetPct_i. Trade_i = Target_i - Current_i.
