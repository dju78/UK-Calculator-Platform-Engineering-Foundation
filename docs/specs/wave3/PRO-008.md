## Purpose

Compare monthly payments, total deal cost, and break-even interest rates between a fixed-rate mortgage and a base-rate tracker mortgage.

## Scope

Capital-and-interest amortising residential and buy-to-let mortgages in the UK comparing fixed deals against tracker margins.

## Assumptions

- Monthly amortisation applies across full term.
- Fixed rate remains constant during deal period.
- Tracker rate equals Bank of England Base Rate plus lender margin.
- Deal period is typically 2, 3, or 5 years.
- Fees can be paid upfront or financed into the loan.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Loan amount must be strictly positive and <= £25,000,000.
- Term must be between 1 and 40 years.
- Deal period must be between 1 and min(term, 10) years.
- Interest rates and margins must be non-negative.

## Formula and logic

Standard annuity formula P = L*r/(1-(1+r)^-n). For tracker with dynamic trajectory, monthly interest is computed step-by-step. Break-even average base rate is solved where Total Deal Cost(Tracker) = Total Deal Cost(Fixed).

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- Zero product fees on both options.
- Equal fixed and tracker rates with zero rate changes.
- Base rate falling sharply vs rising sharply.
- Fees added to loan balance vs paid upfront.
