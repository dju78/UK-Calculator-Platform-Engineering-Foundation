## Purpose

Calculate UK Capital Gains Tax liability on residential and buy-to-let property sales for 2026/27.

## Scope

UK residential property disposals by individuals and joint owners, including Private Residence Relief (PRR) and 60-day reporting.

## Assumptions

- Tax year 2026/27 residential CGT rates: 18% basic rate band, 24% higher rate band.
- Annual Exempt Amount: £3,000 per individual.
- PRR proportional relief based on occupation months plus 9 months deemed final period.
- Unused basic rate income tax band absorbs capital gains at 18% before 24% applies.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Disposal price must be greater than zero.
- Months as main residence cannot exceed total ownership months.
- Total ownership months must be >= 1.
- Number of owners must be >= 1.

## Formula and logic

Gross Gain = Disposal Price - Acquisition Price - Buying/Selling/Improvement Costs. PRR Relief = Gross Gain * (Qualifying Months / Total Months). Chargeable Gain = max(0, Gross Gain - PRR - Losses - AEA). Gains split across available basic rate band at 18% and remaining at 24%.

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- 100% PRR relief (zero tax).
- Disposal at a loss.
- Gain within £3,000 AEA.
- Joint ownership doubling AEA to £6,000.
