## Purpose

Calculate safe initial withdrawal rates (SWR) for retirement based on asset allocation and time horizon.

## Scope

Sustainable retirement drawdown planning based on empirical research (Bengen / Trinity Study).

## Assumptions

- Initial withdrawal adjusted annually for inflation.
- Real returns based on equity and bond portfolio mix.
- Longer horizons require conservative SWR (3.25% - 3.5%).

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Portfolio value must be > 0.
- Retirement years between 10 and 60.
- Equity allocation between 0% and 100%.

## Formula and logic

Weighted real return R = (Eq% * R_eq + Bd% * R_bd). SWR solved via capital preservation and finite annuity formula.
