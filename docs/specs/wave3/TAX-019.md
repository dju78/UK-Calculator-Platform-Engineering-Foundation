## Purpose

Calculate UK High Income Child Benefit Charge (HICBC) clawback under reformed 2026/27 £60,000-£80,000 rules.

## Scope

UK individuals receiving Child Benefit with Adjusted Net Income exceeding £60,000.

## Assumptions

- Taper starts at £60,000 ANI; 100% charge at £80,000 ANI.
- Rate of charge: 1% per £200 of excess ANI above £60,000.
- 2026/27 Child Benefit: £25.60/wk eldest, £16.95/wk additional.
- Gross pension contributions and gross Gift Aid reduce ANI.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Salary and other income >= 0.
- Children count between 1 and 20.
- Pension and Gift Aid >= 0.

## Formula and logic

ANI = Salary + Other - Pension - (GiftAid * 1.25). If ANI <= 60k: 0%. If ANI >= 80k: 100%. Else: floor((ANI - 60000) / 200) * 1%. Charge = Benefit * Charge%.

- ANI <= £60,000 (£0 charge).
- ANI >= £80,000 (100% charge).
- Pension contribution eliminating charge.
