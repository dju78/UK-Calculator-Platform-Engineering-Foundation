## Purpose

Simulate retirement portfolio drawdown runway, inflation-adjusted withdrawals, fees, and depletion timing.

## Scope

Retirement decumulation planning for investment portfolios and drawdown pension pots.

## Assumptions

- Periodic compounding of net returns.
- Annual or monthly withdrawals increasing with inflation.
- Fees deducted continuously/annually from fund balance.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `initial_balance` | Starting balance (£) | number | 500000 | Starting invested wealth |
| `annual_withdrawal` | Annual withdrawal (£) | number | 20000 | Initial annual drawdown |
| `annual_return` | Expected return (%) | number | 5.0 | Nominal annualized return |
| `inflation_rate` | Inflation rate (%) | number | 2.5 | Annual inflation rate |
| `adjust_for_inflation` | Adjust for inflation? | select | 'true' | Link withdrawals to inflation |
| `management_fee` | Annual fees (%) | number | 0.5 | Platform and fund fees |
| `planning_years` | Planning horizon (years) | number | 30 | Horizon duration |

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Initial balance must be > 0.
- Annual withdrawal must be >= 0.
- Planning years between 1 and 60.

## Formula and logic

Step-by-step month simulation: Balance_t = Balance_{t-1} * (1 + net_monthly_return) - Monthly_Withdrawal_t.

## Outputs

- `portfolio_survived`
- `years_until_depleted`
- `final_balance`
- `total_withdrawn`
- `total_growth_earned`
- `total_fees_paid`
- `average_monthly_income`

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- Zero investment return (pure cash drawdown).
- Perpetual growth (return > withdrawal).
- Early depletion.

## Rules and source dependencies

Mathematical simulation. Non-statutory.

## Source provenance

Actuarial decumulation mathematics, FCA retirement guidance.

## Product boundary

Deterministic simulation. Does not model sequence-of-returns volatility.

## Methodology

Month-by-month balance tracking with annual inflation re-indexing.
