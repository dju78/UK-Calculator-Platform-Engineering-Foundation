## Purpose

Compare net retirement returns of investing via a SIPP vs Stocks & Shares ISA under UK 2026/27 rules.

## Scope

UK retirement tax wrapper optimization for basic, higher, and additional rate taxpayers.

## Assumptions

- SIPP contributions receive upfront tax relief (20% basic at source, higher/additional via tax return).
- ISA funded from net income with 100% tax-free growth and withdrawals.
- SIPP allows 25% tax-free lump sum on drawdown, 75% taxed at retiree marginal rate.
- Annual limits: ISA £20k, SIPP £60k.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Monthly contribution must be > 0.
- Years to invest between 1 and 50.
- Growth rate between 0% and 25%.

## Formula and logic

ISA Pot = FV(r, n, C_net). SIPP Pot = FV(r, n, C_gross). Net SIPP = (0.25 * SIPP) + (0.75 * SIPP * (1 - RetTaxRate)).

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- Higher rate taxpayer dropping to basic rate in retirement.
- Basic rate staying basic rate (SIPP wins via 25% tax-free lump sum).
- Zero investment growth.
