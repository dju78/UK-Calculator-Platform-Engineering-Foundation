## Purpose

Simulate thousands of future investment paths with Geometric Brownian Motion to analyze probability of success and sequence risk.

## Scope

Stochastic simulation for investment accumulation and retirement decumulation.

## Assumptions

- Returns follow log-normal distribution with constant mean and volatility.
- Box-Muller transform with seeded PRNG ensures deterministic, reproducible simulation.
- Contributions added or withdrawals subtracted annually.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Initial investment >= 0.
- Horizon between 1 and 50 years.
- Volatility between 1% and 50%.

## Formula and logic

Simulate S_t = S_{t-1} * exp((mu - 0.5*sigma^2) + sigma*Z) + C - W. Compute 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th, 90th percentiles.
