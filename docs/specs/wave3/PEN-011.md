## Purpose

Calculate FIRE number, runway years to financial independence, savings rate, and projected FIRE age in the UK.

## Scope

Wealth accumulation and milestone planning across LeanFIRE, Standard FIRE, and FatFIRE scenarios.

## Assumptions

- FIRE Number = Desired Retirement Spending / (SWR / 100).
- Savings rate = (Annual Savings / Annual Net Income) * 100.
- Invested assets grow at real investment return above inflation.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- Age between 16 and 90.
- Annual income > 0 and >= current spending.
- SWR between 1% and 10%.

## Formula and logic

Savings = Income - Spending. FIRE_Target = TargetSpending / (SWR/100). Solve month-by-month compounding until Balance >= FIRE_Target.
