## Purpose

Calculate annual UK tax on taxable General Investment Accounts (Dividend Tax, CGT, and Savings Interest Tax).

## Scope

Unsheltered taxable investment accounts for UK resident individuals.

## Assumptions

- 2026/27 allowances: Dividend £500, CGT AEA £3,000, PSA £1,000/£500/£0.
- Dividend rates: 8.75% basic, 33.75% higher, 39.35% additional.
- CGT rates: 18% basic, 24% higher.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `annual_dividends` | Annual dividends (£) | number | 2500 | Dividend income |
| `realised_capital_gains` | Realised capital gains (£) | number | 6000 | Net profit from sales |
| `interest_income` | Interest income (£) | number | 800 | Interest distributions |
| `other_taxable_income` | Other taxable income (£) | number | 55000 | Salary/pension for tax band |
| `capital_losses_brought_forward` | Losses brought forward (£) | number | 0 | Unused CGT losses |

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and isolated handler logic:

- All income and gains inputs must be >= 0.
- Non-finite inputs rejected.

## Formula and logic

Div Tax = max(0, Div - 500) * DivRate. CGT = max(0, Gain - Loss - 3000) * CGTRate. Int Tax = max(0, Int - PSA) * IncomeTaxRate.

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- All income within allowances (£0 tax).
- Basic rate taxpayer with gains spilling into higher band.
- Losses wiping out gains.
