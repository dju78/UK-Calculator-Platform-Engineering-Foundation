## Purpose

Work out the monthly repayment and total cost of an amortising business loan, including an arrangement fee.

## Scope

Fixed-rate, capital-and-interest business loans repaid monthly. Variable rates, drawdown facilities, invoice finance and asset finance are out of scope.

## Assumptions

- A fixed interest rate for the whole term.
- Equal monthly payments with no payment holidays.
- Interest accrues monthly on the reducing balance.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |

| `amount` | Loan amount (£) | number | 50000 | - |

| `years` | Term (years) | number | 5 | - |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.


## Formula and logic

Level payment from the standard annuity formula `P = (A x r) / (1 - (1+r)^-n)` where `A` is the amount financed, `r` the monthly rate and `n` the number of months. Where the fee is financed it is added to `A` and therefore attracts interest; where it is paid up front it is excluded from `A` but still counted in the total cost of credit.

## Outputs

- `monthly_payment`
- `total_repayment`
- `total_interest`
- `total_cost_of_credit`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.
