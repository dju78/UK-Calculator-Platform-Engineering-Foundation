## Purpose

Show what a mortgage payment becomes if interest rates rise.

## Scope

A single rate shock applied to the remaining term.

## Assumptions

- The term and balance are unchanged.
- The stressed rate applies for the whole remaining term.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `balance` | Mortgage balance (£) | number | 200000 | - |
| `current_rate` | Current rate (%) | number | 4.5 | - |
| `remaining_years` | Remaining term (years) | number | 20 | - |
| `rate_increase` | Rate rise to test (percentage points) | number | 2 | Enter 2 to test a rise of two percentage points. |
| `gross_monthly_income` | Gross monthly income (£) | number | 4000 | Optional. Used to show the payment as a share of income. |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.


## Formula and logic

Annuity payment recomputed at the current rate plus the shock.

## Outputs

- `current_monthly_payment`
- `stressed_monthly_payment`
- `monthly_increase`
- `annual_increase`
- `payment_to_income_stressed`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- **Plus 2 points** - {"balance":200000,"current_rate":4.5,"remaining_years":20,"rate_increase":2,"gross_monthly_income":4000}
- **Plus 3 points** - {"balance":250000,"current_rate":3.5,"remaining_years":25,"rate_increase":3,"gross_monthly_income":5000}
- **No change** - {"balance":180000,"current_rate":5,"remaining_years":20,"rate_increase":0,"gross_monthly_income":4500}
- **Rate falls** - {"balance":180000,"current_rate":5,"remaining_years":20,"rate_increase":-1,"gross_monthly_income":4500}
- **No income supplied** - {"balance":300000,"current_rate":4,"remaining_years":30,"rate_increase":2}
