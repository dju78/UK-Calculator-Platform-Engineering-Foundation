## Purpose

Work out the annualised money-weighted return on irregularly dated cash flows.

## Scope

Any series of dated cash flows with at least one negative and one positive value.

## Assumptions

- Actual/365 day count, matching the common spreadsheet definition.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cashflows` | Dated cash flows | text | '[{"date":"2024-01-01" | Money out is negative, money in is positive. Needs at least one of each. |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.
- Cash flows that never change sign are rejected.
- Where no root exists in the searched range, a validation message is returned rather than a plausible-looking number.

## Formula and logic

The rate r where sum(amount_i / (1+r)^(days_i/365)) = 0.

## Outputs

- `xirr`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- **Simple two flows** - {"cashflows":"[{\"date\":\"2024-01-01\",\"amount\":-10000},{\"date\":\"2025-01-01\",\"amount\":11000}]"}
- **Irregular contributions** - {"cashflows":"[{\"date\":\"2023-01-15\",\"amount\":-5000},{\"date\":\"2023-07-20\",\"amount\":-3000},{\"date\":\"2025-03-10\",\"amount\":9500}]"}
- **Loss** - {"cashflows":"[{\"date\":\"2022-06-01\",\"amount\":-20000},{\"date\":\"2025-06-01\",\"amount\":15000}]"}
- **Multiple inflows** - {"cashflows":"[{\"date\":\"2021-01-01\",\"amount\":-50000},{\"date\":\"2022-01-01\",\"amount\":10000},{\"date\":\"2023-01-01\",\"amount\":15000},{\"date\":\"2024-01-01\",\"amount\":35000}]"}
- **Long horizon** - {"cashflows":"[{\"date\":\"2010-01-01\",\"amount\":-1000},{\"date\":\"2026-01-01\",\"amount\":4000}]"}

## Rules and source dependencies

Not rules-sensitive. No statutory values are used.

## Product boundary

A money-weighted return. It is affected by the timing of your contributions and so is not comparable with a fund's published time-weighted return.

## Methodology

The engine uses a bracketed root search; the oracle solves the same NPV by pure bisection AND asserts that NPV at the root is essentially zero, so the answer is confirmed to be a genuine root rather than a converged guess.
