## Purpose

Work out an emergency fund target and how long it takes to reach it.

## Scope

Cash emergency savings against essential monthly spending.

## Assumptions

- Essential spending is the unavoidable monthly total.
- The fund is held in accessible cash, so no investment growth is assumed.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `monthly_essentials` | Monthly essential spending (£) | number | 1800 | Rent or mortgage, bills, food, transport and other unavoidable costs. |
| `months_of_cover` | Months of cover wanted | number | 3 | - |
| `current_savings` | Current savings (£) | number | 1000 | - |
| `monthly_contribution` | Monthly contribution (£) | number | 250 | - |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.
- Zero essential spending is rejected. A zero contribution reports no completion date rather than an infinite one.

## Formula and logic

`target = monthly essentials x months of cover`; `shortfall = max(0, target - current savings)`; months to target is `ceil(shortfall / monthly contribution)`.

## Outputs

- `target_fund`
- `shortfall`
- `months_covered_now`
- `months_to_target`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.
