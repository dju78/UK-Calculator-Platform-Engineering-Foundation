## Purpose

Project a Junior ISA to the child's eighteenth birthday, and make clear that the money becomes theirs at that point.

## Scope

Annual contributions up to the Junior ISA limit, growth to maturity at 18, from the child's current age.

## Assumptions

- Contributions are made once a year and growth applied after them.
- The account matures at 18 and no withdrawals are made before then.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `current_balance` | Current Junior ISA balance (£) | number | 0 | - |
| `annual_contribution` | Annual contribution (£) | number | 1200 | The limit is £9,000 a year. |
| `annual_growth` | Annual growth (%) | number | 5 | - |
| `child_age` | Child's age now | number | 5 | A Junior ISA matures at 18, when the money becomes the child's to do as they wish. |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.
- The child's age must be from 0 to one year below the maturity age; anything else is rejected with a readable message rather than silently producing a nonsense term.
- Contributions above the annual limit are capped and the user is told.

## Formula and logic

Each year `v = (v + c) G`, where `c` is the capped contribution and `G` one plus the growth rate. The term is the maturity age less the child's current age.

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- **Newborn, full allowance, eighteen years** - {"current_balance":0,"annual_contribution":9000,"annual_growth":6,"child_age":0}
- **Ten-year-old, modest contributions** - {"current_balance":3000,"annual_contribution":1200,"annual_growth":5,"child_age":10}
- **Contribution above the limit is capped** - {"current_balance":0,"annual_contribution":12000,"annual_growth":4,"child_age":5}
- **Final year before maturity** - {"current_balance":20000,"annual_contribution":9000,"annual_growth":3,"child_age":17}
- **Zero growth isolates the contributions** - {"current_balance":1000,"annual_contribution":500,"annual_growth":0,"child_age":8}
- **Existing pot with no further contributions** - {"current_balance":15000,"annual_contribution":0,"annual_growth":7,"child_age":4}

## Product boundary

The money in a Junior ISA belongs to the child. They take control of the account at 16 and can withdraw the whole balance at 18, whatever it was saved for, and nothing can be withdrawn before then except in tightly limited circumstances. That is a product fact the projection states rather than a risk it can model. Not investment advice.
