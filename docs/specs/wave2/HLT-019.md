## Purpose

Work out gestational age, trimester and estimated due date.

## Scope

The first day of the last period and an average cycle length.

## Assumptions

- Naegele's rule, adjusted for cycle length.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `last_period_date` | First day of your last period | text | "2026-02-01" | In the form YYYY-MM-DD. |
| `cycle_length` | Average cycle length (days) | number | 28 | The classical due date rule assumes 28 days; this adjusts for yours. |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.
- A cycle length outside 20 to 45 days is refused with advice to speak to a GP or midwife.
- A future date, or one more than 45 weeks ago, is refused.
- A malformed date is refused with the expected format.

## Formula and logic

280 days from the first day of the last period, PLUS the difference between the stated cycle length and 28. The classical rule assumes a 28-day cycle, so for a 35-day cycle it is a week out.

## Outputs

- `estimated_due_date`
- `conception_date_estimate`
- `first_trimester_ends`
- `second_trimester_ends`
- `cycle_length_used`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- **Standard 28-day cycle** - {"last_period_date":"2026-02-01","cycle_length":28}
- **A 35-day cycle moves the due date a week later** - {"last_period_date":"2026-02-01","cycle_length":35}
- **A 21-day cycle moves it a week earlier** - {"last_period_date":"2026-02-01","cycle_length":21}
- **Early in the first trimester** - {"last_period_date":"2026-05-20","cycle_length":28}
- **Second trimester** - {"last_period_date":"2026-01-10","cycle_length":28}
- **Late pregnancy** - {"last_period_date":"2025-12-01","cycle_length":28}

## Rules and source dependencies

Not rules-sensitive. No statutory values are used.

## Product boundary

Only about one baby in twenty five arrives on the estimated date, and a dating scan is more accurate than any calculation. Not a test and not a diagnosis.

## Methodology

Three benchmark cases share a last period date and differ only in cycle length, so the adjustment must move the due date by exactly the difference. Date-relative outputs such as gestational age are deliberately NOT benchmarked, because the browser parity harness cannot pin a clock; they are covered by unit tests with a fixed context instead.
