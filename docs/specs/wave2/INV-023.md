## Purpose

Compare reinvesting dividends against taking them as cash.

## Scope

A single holding over a period of years.

## Assumptions

- Dividends are reinvested at the prevailing share price each year.
- Both sides experience the same price growth, so the difference is purely compounding of income.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `initial_investment` | Initial investment (£) | number | 20000 | - |
| `starting_yield` | Starting dividend yield (%) | number | 4 | - |
| `price_growth` | Annual share price growth (%) | number | 5 | - |
| `dividend_growth` | Annual dividend growth (%) | number | 4 | - |
| `years` | Years | number | 10 | - |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.


## Formula and logic

Modelled in SHARES: each year's income buys additional shares at the grown price.

## Outputs

- `final_value`
- `final_value_without_reinvestment`
- `reinvestment_benefit`
- `total_dividends`
- `final_shares`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
