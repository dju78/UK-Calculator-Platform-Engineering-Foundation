## Purpose

Work out Land Transaction Tax on property in Wales.

## Scope

## Assumptions

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.


## Formula and logic

Progressive LTT bands. Additional properties use a SEPARATE higher-rate band table, not a surcharge added to the main rates.

## Rules and source dependencies

LTT main and higher residential bands from the versioned ruleset, verified against gov.wales. The higher rates in force are those effective from 11 December 2024.

## Product boundary

LTT applies to Wales ONLY. Wales has NO first-time buyer relief; the main residential nil-rate band of 225,000 already applies to all main-residence purchases.

## Methodology

Both band tables re-typed from gov.wales into the oracle. A general rates page still showed pre-December-2024 higher rates; the ruleset was verified against the Welsh Government announcement rather than changed to match the stale page.
