## Purpose

Compare a geared property investment against an ungeared stock portfolio.

## Scope

Like-for-like on cash committed over a holding period.

## Assumptions

- The same upfront cash and the same net periodic outlay go into the portfolio.
- Constant growth and return rates.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.


## Formula and logic

Property net gain is end equity less total cash invested. The portfolio starts with the same upfront cash and receives the property's net monthly outlay, compounded monthly.

## Outputs

- `property_final_equity`
- `property_total_invested`
- `property_net_gain`
- `stocks_final_value`
- `stocks_total_invested`
- `stocks_net_gain`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.
