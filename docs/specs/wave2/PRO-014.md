## Purpose

Work out affordable rent, the income a landlord will require, and the upfront cost.

## Scope

UK assured shorthold tenancies.

## Assumptions

- Agents commonly require annual income of about 30x the monthly rent; the multiple is editable.
- Deposit expressed in weeks of rent.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gross_monthly_income` | Gross monthly income (£) | number | 3000 | - |
| `income_multiple` | Income multiple required | number | 30 | Agents commonly require annual income of 30x the monthly rent. |
| `proposed_rent` | Proposed monthly rent (£) | number | "" | Optional. Leave blank to see the maximum rent your income supports. |
| `deposit_weeks` | Deposit (weeks of rent) | number | 5 | Capped at five weeks in England where annual rent is under £50,000. |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.


## Formula and logic

Affordable rent is annual income divided by the multiple. Deposit is weekly rent (annual rent / 52) times the number of weeks.

## Outputs

- `affordable_rent_by_ratio`
- `annual_income_required`
- `rent_to_income`
- `deposit_required`
- `upfront_cost`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.
