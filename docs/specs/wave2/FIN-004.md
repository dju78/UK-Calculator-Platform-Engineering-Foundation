## Purpose

Work out the repayments on a loan secured against property, and the resulting combined loan-to-value.

## Scope

Fixed-rate second-charge and secured personal loans repaid monthly, plus a combined LTV against an existing mortgage.

## Assumptions

- A fixed interest rate for the whole term.
- The existing mortgage balance and property value are as at today.
- Equal monthly payments.

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.
- Property value of zero suppresses the combined LTV rather than dividing by zero.

## Formula and logic

Same annuity formula as FIN-003. Combined LTV is `(existing mortgage + new loan) / property value`.

## Outputs

- `monthly_payment`
- `total_interest`
- `total_cost_of_credit`
- `combined_ltv`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.
