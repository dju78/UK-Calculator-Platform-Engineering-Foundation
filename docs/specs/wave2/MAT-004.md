## Purpose

Apply one or two successive discounts and show what they really come to.

## Scope

An amount with a first discount and an optional further discount.

## Assumptions

- The second discount is applied to the already reduced amount, as shops apply it.

## Inputs

| Name | Label | Type | Default | Units / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `original_price` | Original amount | number | 100 | Any amount, not only a price, which is why no currency symbol is shown. |
| `discount` | Discount (%) | number | 20 | - |
| `second_discount` | Further discount (%) | number | 0 | Taken off the already reduced price, not off the original. |

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.
- Each discount must be between 0% and 100%; a negative amount is refused.

## Formula and logic

Final = amount x (1 - first) x (1 - second). The effective single discount is the total saving over the original amount.

## Outputs

- `price_after_first`
- `final_price`
- `total_saving`
- `effective_single_discount`
- `sum_of_discounts_would_have_been`

Formatting is decided by the central registry in
`apps/web/src/components/calculators/outputFormats.ts`, per calculator and per
key, so ratios render as percentages and money as `£1,234.56`.

## Edge cases covered by benchmarks

- **Single discount** - {"original_price":100,"discount":20,"second_discount":0}
- **Two 20% discounts are 36% off, not 40%** - {"original_price":100,"discount":20,"second_discount":20}
- **Sale price then staff discount** - {"original_price":249.99,"discount":30,"second_discount":10}
- **Full discount leaves nothing to pay** - {"original_price":80,"discount":100,"second_discount":0}
- **No discount at all** - {"original_price":55,"discount":0,"second_discount":0}
- **Half price then half again** - {"original_price":200,"discount":50,"second_discount":50}

## Rules and source dependencies

Not rules-sensitive. No statutory values are used.

## Product boundary

Two 20% discounts are 36% off, NOT 40%: the second is taken from an already reduced price. Shops advertise 'an extra 20% off sale prices' precisely because it sounds like more than it is, so both the real figure and the naive sum are shown side by side. The calculator is unit-agnostic and shows no currency symbol.

## Methodology

Independently derived, with a case pinning the 20-then-20 result at exactly 36%.
