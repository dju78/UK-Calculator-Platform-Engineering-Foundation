## Purpose

Appraise a rental property including purchase costs, yields and cash-on-cash return.

## Scope

Single buy-to-let purchase with an interest-only or repayment mortgage.

## Assumptions

- Vacancy expressed as a percentage of gross rent.
- Purchase tax at additional-property rates.

Percentages are entered as human percentages (5 means 5%) and normalised once
inside the engine.

## Validation

Enforced centrally at the engine boundary and by the shared validation module:

- Any non-finite input is rejected with a readable message.
- Any non-finite output is rejected rather than returned, so `NaN` and
  `Infinity` can never reach a user.
- Terms are bounded (maximum 150 years) and monetary amounts capped, so no
  input can spin an amortisation loop indefinitely.


## Formula and logic

Effective rent is gross rent net of vacancy; NOI deducts running costs; cash-on-cash divides pre-tax cash flow by cash invested.

## Outputs

- `net_yield`
